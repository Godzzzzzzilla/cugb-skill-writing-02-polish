# Results

To evaluate the pretreatment effect, we ran a series of experiments. The samples were
stored at 4 °C for 12 h before measurement, not at room temperature. Because the
instrument drifted during the run, the values in Table 2 may be slightly lower than the
true values. The treatment group showed a 23.4% increase in yield (p = 0.03) over the
control, consistent with earlier work [2,5]. This effect is likely caused by the larger
surface area. The sample size was small (n = 6), so the result may not generalize to
other catalysts.
