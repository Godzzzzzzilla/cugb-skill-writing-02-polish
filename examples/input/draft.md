# Results

In order to evaluate the effect of the pretreatment, we performed a series of experiments.
It is important to note that the samples were not stored at room temperature; they were
kept at 4 °C for 12 h before the measurement. Due to the fact that the instrument drifted
during the run, the values reported in Table 2 may be slightly lower than the true values.
The treatment group showed a 23.4% increase in yield (p = 0.03) compared with the control,
which is consistent with previous work [2,5]. We believe that this effect is likely caused
by the increased surface area. It should be noted that the sample size was small (n = 6),
and therefore the result may not generalize to other catalysts.
